How XP works

  • Planned meal eaten: +10 XP
  • Swap within 10% of the planned protein: +6 XP
  • Daily target hit: +25 XP. Going past it earns nothing extra.
  • Every slot eaten as planned: +15 XP Clean Sweep
  • Streak: days at 90% or more of target. Every 7 streak days earns a Banana Freeze (hold up to 3) that covers one missed day.
